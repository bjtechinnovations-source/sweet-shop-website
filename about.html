Our Story

Celebrating Tradition, Taste, and Trust for Over Three Decades

Our Journey of Taste & Tradition Since 1990

Oberoi Bakers began its journey in 1990 with a simple yet heartfelt vision — to deliver homemade-style taste and pure sweets prepared with honesty and care. Founded by Sardar Joginder Singh and Mr. Gurvinder Singh, the bakery started as a small family-run shop built on trust, dedication, and traditional values.

What began as a modest neighborhood bakery gradually became a trusted name among generations of families. Our commitment to freshness, consistency, and authentic Indian flavors has remained unchanged over the years.

Even today, every sweet and bakery item is prepared using traditional methods, pure ingredients, and strict hygiene standards. We strongly believe that true taste comes from patience, passion, and craftsmanship.

From classic Indian sweets to freshly baked biscuits, cakes, and rusks, each product reflects our heritage and commitment to excellence. At Oberoi Bakers, every bite carries the warmth of tradition and the promise of quality.
